## 2. Session 1 — the EDA build (2026-08-03)

### What was asked and what changed in the asking

The task was to assess Plan 3.0 and design a thorough EDA answering **SQ1** (characterise the
Befæstelsesdata task as a statistical-learning problem). Three decisions were put to the author
before planning, and all three came back at the maximal option:

1. Channel scan scope → **full pool**, all 19,314 tiles, not a sample.
2. Label-quality depth → **full audit plus boundary-ambiguity measurement**.
3. Per-route confusion matrices → **build inside the EDA**, not deferred to Part B.

The first plan was rejected with three binding constraints, which now govern the folder:

- **Additive only.** Nothing already attained may be deleted or modified.
- Output goes in a **new top-level folder**, `exploratory_data_analysis/`, not `logs_and_models/`.
- Structured so **git** can carry the important results within GitHub's 100 MB per-file ceiling.

### Four defects found before the EDA even ran

These came out of reading the code, not from the analysis, and two are live corrections to plan
documents:

1. **DSM and DTM are normalised with placeholder constants.** `mean=0.5, std=1.0` against tiles
   holding raw metres above sea level.
2. **The NIR constant is documented as derived from one image.** `sdfi_dataset.py:146` reads
   `nir_mean = 0.40779018 #based on a sample size of 1! OBS! UNKNOWN`, and that value propagated
   into every 6ch and 10ch config in the frozen matrix.
3. **Channel provenance conflict.** Plan 2.2 section 7.5 and
   `multi_channel_dataset_creation/README.md` disagreed about which folder is nadir and which is
   oblique. *(Superseded: settled by the 2026-08-24 channel-axis identity correction. The "oblique
   source" label is retired. Do not reopen.)*
4. **DSM/DTM nodata is mapped to sea level.** `ImageBlockReplacement.py:242` turns the `-9999`
   sentinel into `0 m`. Later measured as negligible in incidence (see F9).

### Two corrections of record made by this agent

Both were errors this agent introduced and then fixed. They are recorded because the wrong numbers
were stated aloud before being corrected.

1. **"242x" channel-scaling ratio was wrong.** It came from a single flat tile's *within-tile*
   standard deviation at planning time. The full-pool figure is **19x**. Direction of the finding
   unchanged; magnitude and mechanism now measured.

   The corrected framing is that nDSM carries 21.6 % of its variance in the informative within-tile
   component against DSM's 3.4 %, roughly a sixfold improvement, at a third of the total spread.

### Results worth carrying into the chapter

- **The DSM misregistration is 430.5 m** on the solar tile (dx −402.0, dy +154.0). Every other
  raster for that tile sits at dx=dy=0. `exhibit_as_expected=True`.
- **The solar park is annotated as one surface.** This settles the hedge in section 2.1.1. The
  narrow ground strips between panel rows carry `solceller` continuously. Only two wide service
  corridors are broken out: a gravel track (`grus`, 19,336 px, 1.93 %) and a grass access strip
  (`ubefestet`, 56,035 px, 5.60 %). `solceller` is 894,229 px (89.42 %), `unknown` 30,400 px
  (3.04 %) and sits on the tile margin. The sharper claim than "one polygon" is that **narrow
  inter-row gaps are absorbed into solceller while wide service corridors are traced separately.**
- **nDSM clips 12.31 %** of pixels above the fixed 15 m ceiling on the green roof tile. The figure
  is correct, but that fraction saturates to flat yellow. If the tall blocks matter, raise the
  ceiling; if the point is "buildings are elevated, roads are not", 0 to 15 m is the better ramp.
- **Ignore shares** on the four overlays: green roof 45.68 %, high-ignore 76.67 %, solar 3.04 %,
  urban 16.27 %.
- **One unverified visual observation.** On the green roof tile the main carriageway reads
  *untinted* while the asphalt flanking it is magenta. That scene is 45.68 % unlabelled. This was
  eyeballed, not measured, and should be checked directly before it becomes a claim in the

## 6. Environment defects — the most transferable thing in this file

Three hard problems on this VM, all found the expensive way. Any agent reaching for these will hit
the same wall.

1. **sklearn aborts the process in `ML_sdfi`.** Both
   `LinearDiscriminantAnalysis.fit` and `LogisticRegression` with the lbfgs solver kill the
   interpreter with Windows status **`0xC0000409`**, reproducibly, on a trivial 50,000 x 10 array of
   random numbers. It is a broken LAPACK path, not a data problem, and **a hard abort cannot be
   caught in Python** (`try/except` does not help). E1's probe is therefore implemented directly in
   numpy as `lda_fit_predict`, verified against separated Gaussians in `--selftest`.
2. **matplotlib aborts the process in `multi-channel-env`.** Same `0xC0000409`, inside
   `fig.savefig`, with matplotlib 3.9.4 against numpy 2.0.2. Everything up to and including the
   colorbar works, then savefig kills it. **`ML_sdfi` is the env to use for rendering**, and it
   carries `osgeo.gdal` as well, so the gdal backend is still available rather than falling back to
   rasterio.
3. **git is not installed on the VM.** The `.git` folders in the repos came with the clones. As of
   2026-08-03 there was no `git.exe` anywhere on the machine. `exploratory_data_analysis/` was built
   git-ready (`.gitignore`, size guard documented in `README.md`) but could not be committed from
   here. Plan 3.1's 2026-08-28 correction records the same fact independently.

**Parsing traps**, also found the hard way:

- Every spatial-matrix and `dataset_*` per-epoch training CSV is **malformed**: a second header is
  embedded at line 3 and each epoch appears twice with ragged column counts. Naive `pd.read_csv`
  double-counts. `example_dataset_iter_5\iter_5\logs\iter_5.csv` is the clean-format exception.
- All `report_cache_*` files are `;`-separated with stringified matrices in the last column.
- `spatial_matrix\_monitor_state.json` is **stale** and is not a completion oracle.
