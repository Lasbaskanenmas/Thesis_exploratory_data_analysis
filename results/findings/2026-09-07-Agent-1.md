### Task B — Pipeline defects ✅ 2 of 3 FIXED

Three independent defects were found and characterised by measurement, not by code reading.

| # | defect | scope | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **uint8 cast on float channels.** `sdfi_transforms.py` cast every image to `uint8` before augmentation. DSM went from 48,698 distinct values to **18**; DTM to **3** | train-only; inference clean | ✅ **FIXED 2026-08-15**, verified by re-measuring the batch |
| 2 | **`HorizontalFlip` dropped `split_idx`.** `ItemTransform.__init__` was never called, so a 5 % flip ran on **validation** (measured 11/200) | corrupted validation | ✅ **FIXED 2026-08-15**, re-measured **0/200** |
| 3 | **Channel misregistration.** **671 / 19,314 tiles (3.47 %)** have DSM/DTM georeferenced to different ground. DTM misplaced in 571, DSM in 91, neither in 9. `rgb`/`cir` **0 misaligned** | data-level, all elevation configs | ❌ **NOT FIXED** — zero-filled for nDSM by author decision |

**The fix for #1 is subtler than "use float32".** A blanket float32 cast would have broken
`GaussNoise`: albumentations 1.3.0 intensity ops assume float images are in [0,1], and DSM at
20.5–26.5 m would clip to 0.31–1.00. The fix splits bands — genuinely uint8-lossless bands go
through the uint8 path, everything else is left alone.

**Defect #3 bounds what any elevation arm on this data can achieve.** It is identical across `6ch`
and `6ch_corrected`, so it does not explain the difference between them, but it is a standing
caveat on every elevation result.

**Important for interpreting the frozen matrix:** defects #1 and #2 are *identical* across `6ch`
and `10ch`, and the Orto bands are genuinely uint8. **The `10ch − 6ch` contrast therefore survives
both defects intact** and remains usable — it isolates the oblique source with the defects held
constant.

**Recoverability, established before any edit:** repo committed at **`1a26408`** on
`thesis/spatial-matrix-2026`, pushed to `github.com/Lasbaskanenmas/Thesis_ML_sdfi_fastai2`. A

### Task E — The channel axis ✅ RESOLVED

**Finding F4 was falsified — in the opposite direction from the one predicted.**

| cell | Macro-IoU | macro-F1 | overall acc |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| convnext `rgb_ndsm` | **0.3625** | 0.4639 | 0.9337 |
| convnext `rgb` (frozen) | 0.3586 | 0.4593 | 0.9336 |
| convnext `6ch` broken (frozen) | 0.3374 | 0.4258 | 0.9275 |
| convnext **`6ch_corrected`** | **0.3110** | 0.3975 | 0.9267 |
| resnet34 `rgb` (frozen) | 0.2695 | 0.3454 | 0.8973 |
| resnet34 `6ch` broken (frozen) | 0.2477 | 0.3134 | 0.8977 |
| resnet34 **`6ch_corrected`** | **0.2025** | 0.2594 | **0.8797** |

Correcting the normalisation made **both** models **worse**, at both ends of the capacity range.
The mechanism is not that elevation is uninformative — it is that **absolute elevation is a spatial
confound under route blocking**, and the encoding bug was *muting* it:

1. DSM carries **96.6 %** of its variance *between* tiles, DTM **99.6 %** — that is a location code,
   and route blocking puts held-out routes in different places by construction.
2. In the frozen runs those channels reached the network at an effective std of **0.093 / 0.086**
   against RGB's 0.868, *and* truncated to integer metres. The network could largely ignore them.
3. **nDSM is the control.** Clamped, it carries only **48.0 %** between-tile variance. `rgb_ndsm`
   scores 0.3625 vs `rgb` 0.3586 — inside noise. **Remove the location code and the penalty
   disappears, but no gain appears either.**

**The honest statement to KDS** is not "the auxiliary channels are useless". It is: *elevation as
currently supplied encodes location more than structure, and under a spatially honest protocol that
makes it actively harmful; supplying object height instead is safe but did not help on this data.*

Full write-up with the per-fold confound exhibit and the `betonflade` collapse:
**`2026-08-19_channel_axis_findings.md`**.

## 4. The finding most likely to change a conclusion

**Route-level and pooled metrics disagree in direction.**

`convnext_upernet_6ch_corrected` has a **higher** route median macro-IoU (**0.4982**) than
`convnext_upernet_rgb` (**0.4902**) — the *opposite* of the pooled ordering (0.3110 vs 0.3586).

This is not a contradiction. Route-level macro-IoU averages only the classes **present in that
route**, so a class that collapses to ~0 almost everywhere — `betonflade` — is **dropped** from
routes that lack it rather than scored 0. The pooled metric counts it in full.

**This matters because the route level is what the Wilcoxon tests.** Which quantity is tested
decides the direction of a headline result. This agent flagged it in §6 of the channel-axis file
rather than resolving it, because it is a declaration decision, not a computation.

**[CLOSED ELSEWHERE]** — `2026-08-25_pre_declarations.md` §0 names this exact inversion as a
"steering-relevant fact" seen before D3 was written, and locks the per-route metric definition. That
is the right place to look for how it was settled.

The **operative** definition, as implemented in `eda_route_cell_metrics.py:184-193` and asserted by
its selftest: macro-IoU over the classes **present in that route**, absent classes **dropped, not
scored 0**, `ignore_index=0`, `unknown2` report-only. Routes are therefore **not comparable to each
other** — only **between models, paired route by route**, which is what the Wilcoxon needs.
