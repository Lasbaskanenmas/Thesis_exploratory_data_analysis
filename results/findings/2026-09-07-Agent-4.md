## 3. Findings, in brief

Full detail is in `C:\thesis\logs_and_models\contamination_decision\DECISION.md`. This is the

### Task 1 — remedy (c) is executable with zero further compute

Per-tile out-of-fold prediction rasters **were** persisted, at
`logs_and_models\spatial_matrix\<model>\<job>\models\example_dataset\*.tif` (1000x1000 uint8, one
per held-out tile). 560,109 prediction rasters exist across all jobs. Rescoring is arithmetic on
existing files; **no inference was needed and none was run.**

**There are 28 scoreable cells, not 24** — the 24 in `matrix_results_all_cells.csv` plus four that
were scored but never published (§5.2). All 28 were rescored.

**Method.** A pooled confusion matrix is a sum over tiles, so
`CM_clean = CM_all − Σ(contaminated tiles) CM_tile`. `CM_all` came from each cell's persisted
`global_confusion_matrix`; only the 1,281 contaminated tiles were re-read. Exact, not approximate.
Metrics came from the frozen scorer `analyse/per_category_metrics.py` so definitions are identical
to those that produced the published figures.

**Verification that licenses the shortcut:** for `segformer_b1_rgb` the full pooled matrix was
recomputed from disk tile by tile (19,314 tiles, 1,006 s) and compared with the stored matrix —
**identical, max abs diff 0**. Also: no `CM_clean` entry went negative in any cell, and metrics
recomputed from every stored matrix reproduce the published `macro_iou` exactly.

### Task 2 — the effect is material

Over the 24 published cells, scoring on 18,033 clean tiles instead of 19,314:

- **Macro-IoU falls in all 24 cells.** Mean **−0.0238**, median −0.0231, range −0.0310 to −0.0171.
  That is **−8.2 % relative**.
- **Overall accuracy does not move** (mean +0.0019; it *rises* in all 24). The headline accuracy
  figure is robust; Macro-IoU is not.
- macro-F1 falls by a mean of −0.0259.
- The four unpublished cells behave the same way (−0.0252 to −0.0383).
- **No cell changes the number of classes entering the macro average**, so the §7.2 absent-class
  rule is never triggered.

**The delta is two classes.** Mean per-class IoU change: solceller **−0.111**, grus **−0.047**,
asfalt −0.022, fliser −0.022, drivhus −0.013. green_roof, brosten, betonflade and ubefestet are
flat to three decimals.

**The ranking survives.** Best cell is `convnext_upernet_rgb` under both scorings; top five
identical; 11 of 24 cells change rank, none by more than 4 places, all inside the crowded middle.

### Task 2b — is the delta leakage, or just a different population? (added by me)

The work order did not ask this, but it changes how the delta should be read: dropping 1,281 tiles
changes *which ground is scored* (solceller loses 43 % of its tiles), so a Macro-IoU fall is not by
itself proof of leakage. Two controls were run.

- **Route-matched control (the good one).** Four routes contain both contaminated and clean tiles
  (82-20, 84-40, 84-41, 85-45). Within a route, terrain, sensor, flight and held-out model are all
  the same. **In 96 of 96 route x cell combinations the contaminated tiles score higher**, by
  +0.08 to +0.19 accuracy. That is the leakage signature. Caveat: matched, not randomised —
  contaminated tiles are by construction the part of a route another survey also chose to fly.
  84-40's clean side was subsampled to 600 of 5,207 tiles (seed 20260827) for tractability.
- **Shared/unshared window control — INCONCLUSIVE, do not quote.** Splitting each contaminated tile
  into shared and unshared windows gives a Macro-IoU gap of +0.1135 (positive 24/24) but an accuracy
  gap of −0.0373 (negative 24/24). The signs disagree because the shared window is 92.6 % of pixels
  and the unshared remainder is a thin margin at tile edges — not a comparable population. Kept in
  `task2b_shared_window_leakage.csv` for completeness only.

### Task 3 — where the contamination sits

1,281 tiles, **6.63 %** of 19,314, from 6,783 cross-fold pairs. **Every figure in REPORT.md §6.2
reproduced exactly** (212/313/756 by fold; the six-route breakdown).

**The critical answer: 0 of the 90 green_roof tiles are contaminated, and 0 of its 1,430,413
pixels.** Remedy (c) removes none of green_roof's support. The specific worry the work order
raised — that (c) might weaken the class weak GATE 1 was built to protect — **does not
materialise**. green_roof lives only in 84-40 (fold 0, only 1.2 % contaminated overall) and 82-24
(not contaminated at all), and none of the overlap zones carry it.

The other named classes:

| Class | Tiles with class | Contaminated | % lost | Remaining |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| green_roof | 90 | **0** | **0.00 %** | 90 |
| drivhus | 328 | 54 | 16.46 % | 274 |
| betonflade | 598 | 52 | 8.70 % | 546 |
| solceller | 568 | **244** | **42.96 %** | 324 |

**solceller is the real cost of remedy (c)** — 43 % of its tiles and 29.6 % of its pixels, because
all 108 solceller tiles of 85-48 and 109 of 85-45's go, plus 82-20 and 82-21 entirely. It still
spans more than one fold and stays in the macro average, but on a materially thinner base.

The contaminated set is **not representative**: 5.9x enriched in solceller, 3-4x in grus/drivhus/
fliser/asfalt, depleted in ubefestet, betonflade and brosten. It is paved, built ground — the
duplicated routes are re-flights of the same towns.

**Contamination is not marginal at the tile level.** The median cross-fold *pair* shares only
2,370 m² of a 10,000 m² tile, but tiles have several partners: the **union** of shared ground covers
**92.58 %** of all contaminated-tile pixels. Dropping whole tiles is therefore the right unit for (c).

### Task 4 — what remedy (b) would cost

Merging `{82-20, 82-21}` and `{85-45, 85-48}` gives **14 blocking units** (merged sizes 921 and 551
tiles). Exhaustive search over 3^12 with the original checker (green_roof routes split, whales
split, every class spanning >= 2 folds) finds **349,920 feasible assignments** — so **a balanced
split is still constructible, and the binding green_roof constraint is satisfiable**.

Size-optimal pick: fold sizes **6,421 / 6,457 / 6,436**, imbalance **36 tiles (0.19 % of pool)**
against 2 tiles today. Merging does not break the split.

**Compute cost, from the `wandb-summary.json` of every run on disk** (training `_runtime`,
inference `inference_seconds`): rerunning the 24 published cells = 72 fold-runs =
**687.4 GPU-hours** (666.5 h training + 20.9 h inference), mean 9.55 h per fold-run,
= **28.6 days of single-GPU wall clock at 100 % utilisation**. Total already spent across all 90
training and 87 inference runs on disk: 829.4 GPU-hours.

### Task 5 — residual after merging

**Merging the two named pairs does not reach zero**, because a third route pair also shares ground.

| Scenario | Cross-fold pairs | Tiles | % of 19,314 | Imbalance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Frozen split today | 6,783 | 1,281 | 6.63 % | 2 |
| Merged 14 units, size-optimal | **478** | **123** | **0.64 %** | 36 |
| Merged 14 units, best achievable | 0 | 0 | 0.00 % | 181 |

The entire residual is the single pair **84-40 x 84-41**. A zero-residual split **does** exist among
the 14 units — it requires putting 84-40 and 84-41 in the same fold, which the size-optimiser does
not choose on its own; the price is imbalance rising 36 → 181 tiles (0.94 % of pool).

Frozen-split breakdown, for reference: 82-20 x 82-21 = 3,286 pairs, 85-45 x 85-48 = 3,019,
84-40 x 84-41 = 478.

## 4. The recommendation that was given

**Remedy (c)** — keep the frozen split, rescore on the 18,033 clean tiles, publish both numbers —
**presented as a recommendation for the author to weigh, not a decision.**

Reasons: it is already done and costs nothing; the effect is too large (−8.2 % relative, negative
in every cell, corroborated 96/96) for (a) to remain honest; green_roof is untouched so the GATE 1
protection is not weakened; and the model/channel ranking the thesis rests on does not move.

Against it, stated plainly in DECISION.md §7: **(c) does not make the training side clean** — the
fold models were still *trained* on routes duplicating their own held-out ground; only (b) fixes
that. And the clean metric is computed on a population 43 % thinner in solceller, so the two
numbers are not interchangeable.

### 5.1 OPEN AND SERIOUS — 273 tiles where the label and image rasters disagree about location

**For 273 of 19,314 tiles (1.41 %), the geotransform of `labels/splitted_labels/<t>.tif` and the
geotransform of `data/splitted/rgb/<t>.tif` place the same tile at different coordinates** — by
hundreds of metres. Example: `O2021_82_21_1_0005_00001868_0_1000.tif` sits at (498020, 6220655) as
a label and (496820, 6220755) as an image, **1,200 m apart in easting**.

It surfaced because two artefacts silently used different geometry sources:
`tile_inventory.csv` follows the **label** rasters (0 disagreements over all 19,314);
`cross_route_tile_overlaps.csv` follows the **rgb** rasters. The visible symptom was 199 of the
6,783 cross-fold pairs having a declared `overlap_m2` that is geometrically impossible under the
label coordinates.

Affected routes span almost the whole dataset (82-21 2.6 %, 82-24 2.2 %, 83-25 1.5 %, ...; only
83-34 and 85-45 are clean). Full list: `logs_and_models\contamination_decision\raw\geotransform_mismatch_tiles.csv`.

**What is established:** the georeferencing *headers* disagree.
**What is NOT established, and is the open question:** whether the *content* of those 273 label
rasters is aligned with the corresponding image content. **If it is not, those tiles are mislabelled
training and evaluation data.** That is a separate investigation. Per work order §4 ("if anything
contradicts a locked design decision, stop and report rather than acting") it was reported, not
acted on.

**This did not affect any conclusion in this work order** — Tasks 2/3 use tile identity and fold
membership, Task 5 uses route membership, and the route-matched control uses neither. Only the
supplementary shared-window test used coordinates, which is why it covers 1,262 of 1,281 tiles.

**Recommendation for whoever picks this up:** open a small work order to compare label content
against image content for those 273 tiles, and to decide which raster's geotransform is
authoritative. Check the other three agents' handoffs first — label quality was being worked
elsewhere (see `2026-08-24_E4_label_quality.md` in this folder).

## 9. Operational notes for the next session

Practical things that cost this session time and need not cost the next one any.

- **Python.** `C:\thesis\envs\ML_sdfi\python.exe` works directly. numpy 1.26.4, PIL 12.2.0,
  **rasterio is available** (needed for geotransforms; PIL cannot read them). Call the exe by full
  path; no conda activation needed.
- **Permissions.** Project settings (`.claude\settings.json`) **deny the `Write` and `Edit` tools on
  `logs_and_models\**`**. The deliverable was therefore produced by the analysis scripts writing
  their own outputs — the same mechanism every prior artefact in that tree used. PowerShell
  `New-Item`/`Copy-Item` into that tree is also sandbox-blocked; `dangerouslyDisableSandbox` plus
  Python `shutil` worked. **If future work is meant to write reports there, consider a narrow
  allow-rule for the specific output subdirectory.** Writing to
  `exploratory_data_analysis\results\findings\` is not restricted.
- **Timings, measured.** Rescoring one cell over the 1,281 contaminated tiles ≈ 70 s. Full pooled-CM
  recompute for one cell over all 19,314 tiles ≈ 1,006 s. Route-matched control ≈ 170 s per cell.
  Header-only geotransform scan of all 19,314 tiles ≈ 15 min.
- **A method worth reusing.** Any "rescore on a tile subset" question is a **subtraction on the
  stored `global_confusion_matrix`**, not a re-run. It is exact and ~15x cheaper than rescoring the
  complement. `raw\scripts\rescore.py` is the reusable implementation, including the negative-entry
  guard and the full-recompute verification that licenses it.
- **A trap.** `run_spatial_matrix.cmd` is the authoritative cell → prediction-folder map; parse it
  rather than inferring paths from cell names (the `_unw` suffix comes *after* `_fold<k>`, and
  `6ch_corrected` / `rgb_ndsm` break naive patterns).
- **Another trap.** `tile_inventory.csv` and `cross_route_tile_overlaps.csv` disagree on tile
  geometry for 273 tiles (§5.1). Do not mix them in one calculation without checking.
